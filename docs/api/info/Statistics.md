Statistics v2\n\nThe v2 statistics API methods allow you to get various statistics for advertising objects.\n\nThe v2 statistics API methods allow you to get various statistics for advertising objects.\n\nRestrictions and filters in these methods are set using GET parameters, not in the address body, as in the V1 API.\n\n- **[Error handling](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#errors)**\n- **[General statistics](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#statistics)**\n- **[General statistics with pagination](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#statisticsv3)**\n- **[Statistics for the goals](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#goals)**\n- **[Statistics for in-app events](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#inapp)**\n- **[Offline conversions](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#offline_conversions)**\n- **[Fast statistics](https://ads.vk.com/en/doc/api/info/Statistics#faststat)**\n\n## Request restrictions\n\n- Statistics split by day are not returned for more than the last 365 days.\n- You can get statistics for up to 200 objects per request.\n\n## Error handling\n\nIn case of an error, a response like this is returned:\n\n|     |\n| --- |\n| `{`<br>`В  В for the entire time of rollback or daily statistics for the selected period for accounts, campaigns, and banners.\n\nRestrictions and filters are set using GET parameters:\n\n|     |     |     |     |\n| --- | --- | --- | --- |\n| **Parameter** | **Format** | **Default value** | **Description** |\n| date\\_from | YYYY-MM-DD |  | Start date. Only for day.json. |\n| date\\_to | YYYY-MM-DD |  | End date (inclusive). Only for day.json. |\n| id | comma-separated list of IDs |  | List of IDs of banners, campaigns, or users. |\n| metrics | comma-separated list of text IDs | base | List of sets of metrics. Available option: all, base, events, video, uniques, tps, playable, romi. |\n| attribution | impression | default | to attribute at the time of the event, the time of the impression. Ignored for summary |\n\nAll parameters except metrics are required.\n\nYou can get all statistical metrics or specific datasets in a single query.\n\n#### 1\\. base - basic metrics:\n\n- shows - number of impressions;\n- clicks - number of clicks;\n- goals - the number of goals achieved (goals [Top@Mail.ru](mailto:Top@Mail.ru) for websites and mobile app installs);\n- spent - write-offs;\n- cpm - average deduction per 1000 views;\n- cpc - the average write-off in 1 click;\n- cpa - the average write-off for achieving 1 goal;\n- ctr - the percentage of the number of clicks to the number of views;\n- cr - the percentage of the number of completed goals to the number of clicks.\n\n#### 2\\. events - metrics for advertised messages in the social media feed:\n\n- opening\\_app - number of openings of the advertised social network app;\n- opening\\_post - the number of openings of the advertised message in the social media feed;\n- moving\\_into\\_group - the number of clicks on the group page from the advertised message;\n- clicks\\_on\\_external\\_url - number of clicks on the external link in the advertised message;\n- launching\\_video - the number of launches the video in the advertised message;\n- comments - the number of clicks per Comment button;\n- joins - the number of joins to the group via the advertised message;\n- likes - the number of likes of the advertised message;\n- shares - the number of \"Share\" actions for the advertised message;\n- votes - the number of voting actions in the advertised message.\n\n#### 3\\. unique metrics for the number of unique users:\n\n- reach - obsolete metric, now it's always 0;\n- total - the number of unique users who saw ads from the selected campaign from the start of the advertising campaign at the end of the selected period;\n- increment - increase in the number of unique users for the selected period;\n- initial\\_total - the number of unique users who saw ads from the selected campaign from the start of the advertising campaign at the beginning of the selected period (not including period\\_date\\_start)\n- frequency - daily ad display frequency for the selected period\n- frequency\\_total - deprecated metric, value is the same as in frequency.\n\nNote: For advertising campaigns with a broadcast pause of more than 90 days, statistics on unique users will not be relevant.\n\n#### 4\\. video-metrics for video ads:\n\n- started - number of starts of video playback;\n- paused-the number of pauses in video playback;\n- resumed\\_after\\_pause - the amount of video playback after a pause;\n- fullscreen\\_on - the number of times the full-screen video playback mode is enabled;\n- fullscreen\\_off - the number of times the full-screen video playback mode is turned off;\n- sound\\_turned\\_off - the number of video sound offs;\n- sound\\_turned\\_on - the number of video audio inclusions;\n- viewed\\_10\\_seconds - number of views of the first 10 seconds of the video;\n- viewed\\_25\\_percent - number of views of the first 25% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_50\\_percent - number of views of the first 50% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_75\\_percent - number of views of the first 75% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_100\\_percent - number of views 100% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_10\\_seconds\\_rate-percentage of views with the first 10 seconds of video reached;\n- viewed\\_25\\_percent\\_rate-percentage of views with the first 25% of the video duration reached;\n- viewed\\_50\\_percent\\_rate-percentage of views with the first 50% of the video duration reached;\n- viewed\\_75\\_percent\\_rate-percentage of views with the first 75% of the video duration reached;\n- viewed\\_100\\_percent\\_rate - the percentage of hits with the achievement of 100% of the video duration;\n- depth\\_of\\_view - average video viewing depth (as a percentage);\n- view\\_10\\_seconds\\_cost - average cost of viewing the first 10 seconds of video;\n- viewed\\_25\\_percent\\_cost - average cost of viewing the first 25% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_50\\_percent\\_cost - average cost of viewing the first 50% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_75\\_percent\\_cost - average cost of viewing the first 75% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_100\\_percent\\_cost - the average cost of viewing 100% of the video duration.\n\n#### 5\\. carousel-statistics for individual slides of the advertising carousel (N - from 1 to the number of slides):\n\n- slide\\_N\\_shows - number of slide impressions N;\n- slide\\_N\\_clicks - number of clicks on the slide N;\n- slide\\_N\\_ctr-percentage of the number of clicks to the number of views on the slide N;\n\n#### 6\\. tps - statistics on the additional write-offs:\n\n- tps-additional charges for using the moat service;\n- tpd-additional charges for using third-party data (from dmp).\n\n#### 7\\. moat-statistics based on moat service data:\n\n- impressions - number of impressions;\n- in\\_view, the number of visible impressions;\n- never\\_focused - number of impressions in an inactive tab;\n- never\\_visible - number of impressions outside the viewport;\n- never\\_50\\_perc\\_visible - the number of impressions with the ad's visibility zone less than 50%;\n- never\\_1\\_sec\\_visible - the number of impressions with a visibility duration of less than 1 second;\n- human\\_impressions - the number of verified impressions;\n- impressions\\_analyzed - analyzed the number of impressions;\n- in\\_view\\_percent - the percentage of visible impressions;\n- human\\_and\\_viewable\\_perc - the percentage of verified impressions;\n- never\\_focused\\_percent-percentage of impressions in an inactive tab;\n- never\\_visible\\_percent-percentage of impressions outside the viewport;\n- never\\_50\\_perc\\_visible\\_percent-percentage of impressions with the ad's visibility zone less than 50%;\n- never\\_1\\_sec\\_visible\\_percent-percentage of impressions with a visibility duration of less than 1 second;\n- in\\_view\\_diff\\_percent-difference in the number of visible impressions;\n- active\\_in\\_view\\_time - average time when the ad is in the viewport;\n- attention\\_quality - the level of engagement.\n\n#### 8\\. playable - metrics for Playable Ads:\n\n- playable\\_game\\_open-opening the game;\n- playable\\_game\\_close-closing the game;\n- playable\\_call\\_to\\_action-clicks.\n\n#### 9\\. romi-return on investment metrics:\n\n- value - the specified event value\n- romi-return on investment indicator\n- adv\\_cost\\_share-share of advertising expenses\n\n#### 10\\. uniques\\_video - unique metrics for video ads:\n\n- started - number of unique video playback starts;\n- viewed\\_10\\_seconds - number of unique views for the first 10 seconds of the video;\n- viewed\\_25\\_percent - number of unique views for the first 25% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_50\\_percent - the number of unique views of the first 50% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_75\\_percent - the number of unique views for the first 75% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_100\\_percent - number of unique views for 100% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_10\\_seconds\\_rate - percentage of unique views reaching the first 10 seconds of the video;\n- viewed\\_25\\_percent\\_rate - percentage of unique views reaching the first 25% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_50\\_percent\\_rate - percentage of unique views reaching the first 50% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_75\\_percent\\_rate - percentage of unique views reaching the first 75% of the video duration;\n- viewed\\_100\\_percent\\_rate - percentage of unique views that reach 100% of the video duration;\n- depth\\_of\\_view - average depth of unique video views (in percent);\n\nResponse structure:\n\n- items - array of statistics for the requested period\n  - id - object ID\n  - rows - array of statistics for a specific date\n    - date - date\n    - metrics - arrays of requested metrics\n  - total - total object statistics for the requested period\n- total - total statistics for all objects for the requested period\n\nExample
